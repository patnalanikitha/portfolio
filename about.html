Hi, I'm Nikitha Patnala

I am a 3rd-year Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at GITAM University (Class of 2028). My core strength lies in UI/UX & Product Design, coupled with hands-on Full-Stack Engineering.

I believe software should not merely function; it should feel effortless, intuitive, and visually harmonious. I focus heavily on creating deliberate user journeys in Figma, building cohesive design systems, and converting those concepts into pixel-perfect, accessible web interfaces.

Simultaneously, I am deeply intrigued by Local LLMs and AI Agents—exploring how small language models, retrieval pipelines, and AI engineering can be embedded into student tools and productivity workflows like StudyFlow.
