The Concept: Digital Calm & Artisan Craft

Japanese Aesthetics Meets Melbourne Coffee Culture

Komorebi (木漏れ日) is the Japanese word for sunlight filtering through the leaves of trees. In an age of sensory overload and relentless notification loops, Komorebi Café was built as an oasis of deliberate slowness—allowing users to step behind the espresso bar, grind single-origin beans, steam velvety microfoam, and serve drinks to eccentric café visitors against the backdrop of gentle procedural rain.

As a UI/UX designer and full-stack developer, I engineered every visual layer to radiate tactile warmth: handcrafted warm espresso tokens (#2a1d17, #c48c66, #fbf6ee), soft steam physics, and audio synthesized completely on-the-fly without downloading heavy MP3 sound files.

1. Interactive Liquid Physics & Vessel Simulation

Density Math & Visual Foam Dynamics

The crafting canvas simulates realistic beverage construction with reactive visual feedback based on vessel geometry, liquid densities, and temperature states:

  • Artisan Vessel Geometries: Supports authentic glassware including ceramic Demitasse cups, Classic Ceramic Mugs, faceted Gibraltar glasses (for Cortado), ribbed Cold Brew tumblers, and stemmed Dessert glasses.
  • Layered Fluid Dynamics: Visual calculation of liquid height, golden crema meniscus, 1:1 espresso-to-milk division, and dense vanilla gelato sinking dynamics in Affogatos.
  • Topping & Latte Art System: Seamless application of fern rosetta latte art, cocoa dust scatter patterns, Dutch fudge chocolate drizzle, and whipped cream peaks.
  • Procedural Steam Animations: Dynamic CSS blur keyframes simulating rising heat vapors for hot drinks that naturally disappear when cold brew or ice is added.

2. Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer

Zero-Sample Real-Time Sound Synthesis

Komorebi Café does not rely on pre-recorded audio files. Every acoustic sensation is algorithmically generated in real-time through the HTML5 AudioContext API using biquad filters, pink noise buffers, and frequency modulation:

  • Continuous Procedural Rain Generator: Custom pink/brown noise curve filtered through a 950Hz low-pass biquad filter, providing soothing organic rain ambiance with a smooth 1.2s ramp-up.
  • Espresso Extraction Hiss: 110Hz sawtooth wave combined with a 1200Hz bandpass filter simulating high-pressure steam pushing through packed coffee grounds.
  • Liquid Pour & Steam Wand: Frequency-swept lowpass filter (800Hz → 1500Hz) for pouring, and 2400Hz highpass filter with gentle exponential decay for milk steaming.
  • Harmonic Counter Service Bell: Multi-oscillator brass bell chime synthesizing frequencies at 1980Hz, 3120Hz, 4800Hz, and 6200Hz with exponential harmonic attenuation.
  • Ceramic Cup Clink: 1620Hz sine wave frequency modulated down to 1400Hz with an 180ms exponential drop for physical ceramic tactile feedback.

3. The Barista Grimoire & Recipe Verification

Specialty Coffee Recipes & Step Validation

Players can consult an interactive recipe grimoire detailing specialty coffee drink specifications, step sequences, and flavor profiles:

Melbourne Flat White
Double ristretto with folded microfoam in a ceramic mug. No stiff bubbles.
Spanish Cortado
Equal 1:1 cut of single espresso and warm milk served in a faceted Gibraltar glass.
Authentic Long Black
Hot water poured first, topped with double espresso to preserve the delicate crema.
Affogato Al Caffè
Cold artisan vanilla gelato drowned in a hot shot of freshly pulled espresso.

4. Pixel Characters & Narrative System

Cozy Stories & Unlockable Barista Stamps

Each café regular has their own animated pixel avatar, background story, and drink preferences:

  • Clara ("The Novelist"): Sitting in her yellow raincoat, lost in antique fantasy novels; rewards the player with the Antique Bookmark Stamp for an unbroken crema Long Black.
  • Milo ("The Cat Enthusiast"): Loves watching raindrops roll down the window; orders a velvety Caffè Latte with fern rosetta latte art to earn the Lucky Calico Stamp.
  • Elena ("The Jazz Pianist"): Between rehearsal sets, craves the temperature contrast of an Affogato; awards the Golden Treble Clef stamp.
  • Kenji ("The Roaster Apprentice"): Tests extraction timings and microfoam quality with a strict Melbourne Flat White order, awarding the Barista Tamper Trophy.
